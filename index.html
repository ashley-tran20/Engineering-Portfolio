A little about me

Hi, I'm Ashley.

I'm an Electrical and Computer Engineering student at Vanderbilt University pursuing a minor in Computer Science. I expect to graduate in May 2028.

I enjoy understanding how things work and figuring out how to make them better. My projects have given me experience with microcontrollers, mechanical design, electrical circuits, software development, virtual reality, and machine learning.

I'm particularly interested in robotics and assistive technology because of their potential to improve people's everyday lives. I enjoy projects where I can take an idea through the process of designing, building, testing, and improving a working system.

Outside of my projects, I work as an undergraduate research assistant and a teaching assistant for Intermediate Software Design. I also serve as Vice President of the Society of Asian Scientists and Engineers and participate in Vanderbilt Women in Computing.
